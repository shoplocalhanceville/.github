# Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping

Local news, entertainment, shopping & community guide for Hanceville and Cullman County, Alabama.

**Visit the site:** https://shoplocalhanceville.github.io/
**Contact:** shoplocalhanceville@gmail.com
**YouTube:** [@shoplocalhanceville](https://www.youtube.com/@shoplocalhanceville)

## What's here

- **[shoplocalhanceville.github.io](https://github.com/shoplocalhanceville/shoplocalhanceville.github.io)** — the live website: a verified directory of 767 local businesses, events, places, and community resources for Hanceville and Cullman County, hand-built and maintained to a 100-point Lighthouse standard on every page.
- **alabamatownhub** (private) — the Alabama Town Hub build in development.

## About

Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping is an independent community project. It is not the official Chamber of Commerce and is not affiliated with the City of Hanceville. Listings are free for local businesses, and every listing is verified against the business's own sources before it publishes.
